# Rockwell Automation Celebrates the Fifth Annual PartnerNetwork Award Winners at the 2026 PartnerNetwork Conference  | Rockwell Automation | US

Source: https://www.rockwellautomation.com/en-us/company/news/press-releases/2026-partnernetwork-conference-winners.html

![PartnerNetwork logo](https://www.rockwellautomation.com/content/dam/rockwell-automation/images/logos/ra-brands/16x9/16x9-ra-partner-logos/ra-partner-logos-partnernetwork/16x9-RA-Partner-Logos-PartnerNetwork-cmyk.svg)

MILWAUKEE, Wis., March 19, 2026 – Rockwell Automation, Inc. (NYSE: ROK), the world’s largest company dedicated to industrial automation and digital transformation, announced the winners of the fifth annual PartnerNetwork™ awards during its PartnerNetwork Conference event from March 16 to 18 in Las Vegas, Nevada.

The invite-only event brought together the PartnerNetwork ecosystem from both North America and Latin America to focus on unlocking new opportunities and delivering outcomes that matter, together. The event featured keynote presentations from Rockwell Automation leadership and an insightful guest speaker, open discussions with partner program managers, networking, and breakout sessions focused on topics that matter most to the partner community. By working collaboratively as one team, the toughest of challenges are resolved with unique solutions to help customers achieve more resilience and agility. With the PartnerNetwork awards, Rockwell Automation honors the accomplishments the partner community has achieved and the work the partner community does together.

The Rockwell Automation PartnerNetwork awards recognize and celebrate partner achievements in bringing innovative solutions to customers that help drive operational efficiencies and create new business models or revenue streams. These organizations have exemplified the value of the ecosystem and the benefits of having collective strategic alignment. These winners highlight a significant advantage of collaboration as they have integrated both Rockwell Automation and other PartnerNetwork solutions to help overcome the greatest challenges customers are facing.

“On behalf of Rockwell Automation, I want to extend our thanks and appreciation not only to the award winners, but also to all partners who submitted their achievements from the past year. We continue to deliver market-leading technology with great success thanks to the joint work performed within our PartnerNetwork ecosystem,” said Sidney Sollazo, regional vice president, North America Market Access, Rockwell Automation. “By prioritizing innovation, our customers see how the PartnerNetwork community is focused on serving current needs while also anticipating future ones.”
